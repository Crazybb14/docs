Unblockedgames goes beyond games — it also gives you quick access to streaming sites so you can watch movies and TV shows in the same place you play. Instead of bookmarking separate streaming services, you can jump to them directly from the Unblockedgames homepage in just a couple of clicks.

## Finding Streaming Links

To reach the streaming options, open the category dropdown at the top of the homepage and select **Streaming**. The game grid will filter down to show only the streaming entries. Click either link and it will open the streaming site in a new browser tab.

<Tip>
  Use the category dropdown and select **Streaming** to jump straight to these links without scrolling through the full game grid.
</Tip>
