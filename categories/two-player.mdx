Two-player games let two people compete head-to-head or cooperate side by side — all on the same device, sharing a single keyboard. Whether you're challenging a friend at school or going up against a sibling at home, these games are built for split-keyboard, same-screen fun with no extra setup required.

<Info>
  All two-player games use split keyboard controls on one device — no second controller, no network connection, and no account needed. Just sit down together and start playing.
</Info>
