Idle and clicker games reward patience over reflexes — start small, build up resources, unlock upgrades, and watch your numbers climb. Whether you want something to click furiously during a study break or a game to leave running quietly in the background, these titles offer a satisfying loop that keeps paying off over time.

  <Card title="Cookie Clicker" icon="cookie">

  <Card title="Doge Miner" icon="rocket">
    Mine your way to the moon as the iconic Doge. Click to dig, earn currency, and upgrade your equipment until you have enough rocket power to launch into orbit.

<Tip>
  Idle games save your progress in your browser's **local storage** — your upgrades and resources will still be there when you come back, as long as you don't clear your browser data or switch to a private/incognito window.
</Tip>

<Info>
  These games are designed to run in the background. Feel free to switch to another tab while your upgrades work — come back later to collect your earnings and keep leveling up.
</Info>
