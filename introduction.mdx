## Popular Titles

The Unblockedgames library includes some of the most recognized browser game titles around. **Slope** challenges you to guide a ball down an endless, twisting course at high speed. **Minecraft (EaglerCraft)** brings the full Minecraft experience directly to your browser. **Retro Bowl** delivers an addictive American football management and gameplay experience. **1v1.lol** lets you build and battle in a fast-paced competitive shooter. **Subway Surfers** has you dodging obstacles in an endless runner. Beyond these fan favorites, the library also includes **Temple Run 2**, **SuperHot**, **Run 3**, **Geometry Dash**, and **Bloons TD** — along with dozens of other titles across every genre.

## Game Technology

Unblockedgames supports two types of browser-based games, and both work without installing any plugins or extensions.

**Flash games** — classic titles like Bloons TD and Alien Hominid — are powered by [Ruffle](https://ruffle.rs/), a WebAssembly-based Flash emulator that runs entirely in the browser. Ruffle faithfully recreates the Flash experience without requiring Adobe Flash Player, which is no longer supported by modern browsers.

**Unity WebGL games** — titles like Slope and Biters.io — are built with Unity's WebGL export and run natively in any modern browser. These games typically display a loading progress bar before starting, and deliver smooth, high-quality visuals and gameplay.
