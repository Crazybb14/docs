Subway Surfers is the classic endless runner where you dash along train tracks, vault over oncoming trains, slide under barriers, and collect coins while a relentless inspector gives chase. Unblockedgames features four city editions — **Beijing**, **Houston**, **Monaco**, and **San Francisco** — each with its own visual style and scenery. The core gameplay is identical across all four, so once you learn the mechanics, you can jump into any edition and hit the ground running. Easy to pick up, hard to put down.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Your Edition">
    Pick from the four available city editions: Beijing, Houston, Monaco, or San Francisco. Each is accessible from the Unblockedgames game directory. All four share the same controls and mechanics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Your Run">
    Your character begins sprinting down the tracks automatically. The inspector appears behind you instantly — there's no grace period, so get your fingers on the arrow keys right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dodge Trains and Obstacles">
    Use the **Left and Right Arrow keys** to switch between the three lanes and avoid oncoming trains, barriers, and parked cars. Use the **Up Arrow** to jump over obstacles and the **Down Arrow** to roll under low-hanging barriers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect Coins and Power-Ups">
    Coins are scattered along all three lanes — try to collect them without putting yourself in danger. Power-ups like the **Coin Magnet**, **Jetpack**, **Super Sneakers**, and **Score Multiplier** appear on the tracks and dramatically boost your run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Hoverboards to Survive Crashes">
    If you have a hoverboard active, it absorbs one crash that would normally end your run. Tap to activate it before hitting an obstacle — or save it for when the inspector closes in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Left Arrow | Move to the left lane |
| Right Arrow | Move to the right lane |
| Up Arrow | Jump over an obstacle or train |
| Down Arrow | Roll under a low barrier |

## Tips & Tricks

- **Always grab the Coin Magnet.** It pulls in coins from all three lanes automatically, letting you focus entirely on dodging rather than collecting — and it massively multiplies your coin count per run.
- **Hoverboards save you from one crash — use them before you need them.** Activate your hoverboard when the tracks ahead look dangerous rather than waiting until you've already collided. A crashed hoverboard is still a saved run.
- **Jump early for tall obstacles.** The timing window feels generous at first but becomes tight at higher speeds. If a train or barrier is approaching, jump a beat earlier than you think you need to.
- **Roll immediately after landing a jump.** Many obstacle sequences pair a jump barrier with a low barrier right behind it. Get into the habit of pressing Down Arrow right after you land to roll through the second obstacle without breaking stride.
- **Vary your lane position.** Running in the center lane gives you the most options to dodge either way. Avoid hugging one side unless coins or a power-up are lined up there.
