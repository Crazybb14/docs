Temple Run 2 drops you into a breathless sprint through ancient temple ruins as a monstrous demon monkey closes in behind you. Your runner never stops moving — your only job is to keep them alive. Turn at the right moment, jump over roots and fire, slide under overhanging gates, and tilt around ramps as the path twists through rope bridges, mine carts, and crumbling cliffside passages. It's the game that defined the infinite runner genre on mobile, and now it runs right in your browser.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Game">
    Open the game in a new browser tab from the homepage and wait for the game to load. Temple Run 2 runs as an HTML5 game directly in your browser — no plugins or installation needed. Give it a moment to initialize before the menu appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Running">
    Select a character from the main menu and begin your run. Your character moves forward automatically — you control direction, jumping, and sliding only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn at Forks">
    When the path bends left or right, press the **Left or Right Arrow key** to turn. Missing a turn sends your runner off the edge or into a wall. Watch ahead for the path's curve and act early.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jump and Slide Over Obstacles">
    Press **Up Arrow** to jump over roots, fire, and gaps in the path. Press **Down Arrow** to slide under low-hanging gates, vines, and stone arches. Some sections require a jump followed immediately by a slide — stay ready for both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect Coins and Power-Ups">
    Gold coins are scattered along every path. Collect them to spend on power-up upgrades and character unlocks. Power-ups found on the track — like the coin magnet or shield — activate immediately when you run through them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Left Arrow | Turn left at a corner |
| Right Arrow | Turn right at a corner |
| Up Arrow | Jump over an obstacle or gap |
| Down Arrow | Slide under a low obstacle |
| Left Click + Drag | Swipe gesture alternative for turning, jumping, or sliding |

## Tips & Tricks

- **Prioritize turning over collecting coins.** In the first few seconds of a run, it's tempting to grab every coin in sight. But missing a turn ends your run immediately — focus on the path first and collect coins when it's safe.
- **Use power-ups the moment you see them.** Running through a shield or magnet activates it automatically, but upgrades you buy from the shop make those power-ups more powerful and longer-lasting — invest coins there early.
- **React to the path, not the obstacles.** The safest way to read Temple Run 2 is to watch where the track goes, not what's blocking it. If you're always reacting to individual obstacles, you'll be a half-second behind. Track the path first.
- **Memorize common obstacle sequences.** Certain pairings come up repeatedly — a jump immediately followed by a slide, or a turn into a sharp downhill ramp. Recognizing these early lets you chain the inputs smoothly instead of scrambling.
- **Stay in the center of the path on straight sections.** Keeping your runner centered gives you the maximum response time to turn either direction at the next fork.

<Note>
  Temple Run 2 runs as an HTML5 game in your browser. Give it a moment to initialize fully — if the screen appears frozen after 30 seconds, try refreshing the page.
</Note>
