1v1.lol is the browser-based competitive shooter that lets you sharpen the exact skills that win in games like Fortnite — building, aiming, and out-maneuvering opponents — without any download required. You can drop straight into a 1v1 against a real player, grind free-for-all matches, or use the practice zones to refine your build speed and aim. The combination of shooting mechanics and real-time building makes every match a test of both reflexes and game sense, and because it runs entirely in the browser, you can get a match going in seconds.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Game and Choose a Mode">
    Open the game in a new browser tab from the homepage and select a game mode from the lobby. Options include **1v1** (one-on-one ranked), **Box 2v2**, **Battle Royale**, and **Practice** — start with Practice if you're new to the build mechanic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load Into a Match">
    Once a match is found or started, you'll spawn into the arena. Get your bearings quickly — opponents can engage you within the first few seconds, especially in 1v1 mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build Structures for Cover">
    Use your build keys (Z, X, C, V) to place walls, platforms, ramps, and stairs. Building cover mid-fight is the single most important skill in the game — a well-placed wall blocks a shot that would have ended your run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shoot and Eliminate Opponents">
    Aim with your mouse and fire with Left Click. Combine movement, building, and shooting to out-position your opponent. Taking the high ground — by building ramps up — almost always gives you the advantage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Win the Match">
    In 1v1 mode, eliminate your opponent to win the round. In Battle Royale, be the last player standing. Keep an eye on the storm or zone boundary and prioritize staying inside it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Left Click | Shoot / punch |
| Z | Build wall |

| C | Build ramp |
| V | Build stair |
| R | Rotate structure before placing |
| Shift | Sprint |
| Space | Jump |
| F | Edit structure |

## Tips & Tricks

- **Practice building before you fight real opponents.** Use the Practice mode to build ramp-wall combos until it feels automatic. In live matches, you won't have time to think about keybinds — the motion needs to be instinctive.
- **High ground wins fights.** Ramp up aggressively to get above your opponent. Shooting down at someone is a massive mechanical and psychological advantage — they have to aim up while you aim straight down.
- **Aim for headshots.** Headshots deal significantly more damage. Crouch briefly while aiming to tighten your accuracy, especially at medium range.
- **A wall saves your life more than a shot does.** When you take damage, your first reaction should be to place a wall between you and the enemy, not to immediately return fire. Buy yourself a moment to reset.
- **Customize your keybinds.** The default binds aren't optimal for everyone. Head into settings and remap build keys to whatever feels natural on your keyboard — many players prefer keys closer to WASD.
