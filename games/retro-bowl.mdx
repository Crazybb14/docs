Retro Bowl combines the thrill of on-field football with the depth of a team management sim — all wrapped in a charming pixel-art style. You're not just the quarterback: you're the general manager too. Sign players, manage your salary cap, build up your coaching staff, and then step onto the field to throw passes and call runs yourself. The passing mechanic is satisfying and easy to pick up, making Retro Bowl one of the most replayable sports games available in the browser.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a New Franchise">
    Open the game in a new browser tab from the homepage and begin a new franchise. You'll be assigned a struggling team to rebuild. Check your roster and note your players' star ratings and positions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Your Roster">
    Between games, visit the front office to sign free agents, release underperforming players, and spend credits on coaching staff upgrades. Watch your salary cap — going over it penalizes your team's morale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call Plays During Games">
    When a game starts, you play as the offensive coordinator and quarterback. Select your play from the options displayed, then take the snap by tapping or clicking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Throw Passes with Swipe">
    After the snap, swipe from your quarterback toward a receiver to throw the ball. The angle and speed of your swipe determines the throw's trajectory — lead your receiver ahead of their route for the best results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Win Games and Reach the Retro Bowl">
    String together wins to advance through the season. Win your conference and make it to the Retro Bowl — the championship game — to complete your franchise goal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Click / Tap | Select a play, snap the ball, or interact with menus |
| Swipe (direction) | Throw a pass — swipe direction determines trajectory |
| Click and hold | Keep the quarterback running after the snap |
| Click receiver icon | Target that specific receiver (when available) |

## Tips & Tricks

- **Build a strong offensive line first.** A high-rated O-line gives you more time in the pocket to find open receivers — it's the single best investment you can make early in your franchise.
- **Manage your salary cap carefully.** Overspending on stars leaves you with a weak supporting cast. A balanced team with solid role players outperforms a roster of one superstar surrounded by scrubs.
- **Lead your receivers, don't throw directly at them.** Aim your swipe a step or two ahead of your receiver's route so the ball arrives as they do. Throwing behind a receiver almost always results in an incompletion or interception.
- **Short passes beat long bombs in tough situations.** When the defense is pressuring you, a quick slant or flat route gets you yards without risking a turnover. Save the deep ball for when you have a clear mismatch.
- **Upgrade your coaching staff.** Coaches improve your team's performance across the board — better conditioning, better play calling, and improved player development over the course of a season.

<Tip>
  Retro Bowl saves your progress automatically in your browser using local storage — return anytime to continue your season without losing a single game.
</Tip>
