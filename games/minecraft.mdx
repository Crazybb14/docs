EaglerCraft brings the full Minecraft experience directly to your browser — no launcher, no account, no installation required. Built on Minecraft 1.5.2, it includes both Survival and Creative modes, letting you punch trees, craft tools, build towering structures, and fight off creepers just like the original. It even supports multiplayer through EaglerCraft-compatible servers via WebRTC. Whether you want to grind through Survival or spend hours building in Creative, it's all here and it loads in seconds.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Game">
    Open the game in a new browser tab from the homepage and wait for EaglerCraft to initialize in your browser. Click the canvas area to let the game capture your mouse cursor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a New World">
    From the main menu, select **Singleplayer**, then click **Create New World**. Give your world a name, choose your game mode (Survival or Creative), and click **Create New World** to generate the terrain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather Resources in Survival Mode">
    If you chose Survival, start by punching trees to collect wood. Use the wood to craft a crafting table, then build your first set of tools before nightfall — mobs spawn in the dark.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and Explore in Creative Mode">
    In Creative mode, your inventory is unlimited and you can fly freely. Use it to build ambitious structures or explore the world without worrying about health or resources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join Multiplayer (Optional)">
    From the main menu, select **Multiplayer** and enter a compatible EaglerCraft server address. Multiplayer runs over WebRTC, so make sure your browser supports it (most modern browsers do).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| W / A / S / D | Move forward / left / back / right |
| Space | Jump (hold to fly up in Creative) |
| Left Shift | Sneak (hold to fly down in Creative) |
| Left Click | Break block / attack |
| Right Click | Place block / use item |
| E | Open inventory |
| Esc | Open menu / release mouse cursor |
| Scroll Wheel | Cycle hotbar items |
| 1–9 | Select hotbar slot directly |
| F5 | Toggle third-person view |

## Tips & Tricks

- **Always collect wood first in Survival.** Wood unlocks your crafting table, which unlocks every other tool tier. Don't wander far until you have at least a wooden pickaxe.
- **Build a shelter before your first night.** Nights are dangerous — dig into a hillside or build a small hut from your gathered wood. Even a dirt box with a door keeps you safe.
- **Use Creative mode to experiment with builds.** If you're new to Minecraft or just want to create without pressure, Creative lets you try out designs freely before committing to them in Survival.
- **EaglerCraft is based on Minecraft 1.5.2**, which predates features like horses, the End Poem, and many modern biomes. The core gameplay loop is identical, but don't expect 1.20+ content.
- **Press F to toggle between mouse look and cursor release** if you need to interact with the browser outside the game canvas.
