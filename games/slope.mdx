Slope puts you in control of a ball hurtling down a neon-lit 3D slope that never ends — and never slows down. The track twists, narrows, and throws red obstacles in your path, while the speed climbs relentlessly higher. One wrong move sends you off the edge or into a wall, ending your run instantly. It's brutally simple and deeply addictive, which is why Slope remains one of the most-played games on the platform.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Game">
    Open the game in a new browser tab from the homepage and wait for the Unity WebGL bundle to finish loading. The ball will appear on the slope once the engine is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Rolling">
    The ball begins moving forward automatically as soon as the level loads. You don't accelerate — the slope does that for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Steer Left and Right">
    Use the **Left / Right Arrow keys** or **A / D** to steer the ball across the slope. Avoid drifting off the edges and stay clear of the red rectangular obstacles scattered across the path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Survive as Long as Possible">
    The slope speeds up continuously the longer you survive. Your score is based on distance traveled — the further you go, the higher your score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chase Your High Score">
    After each run ends, your score is displayed. Restart immediately and try to beat it. Most improvement comes from the first few minutes of play as you adapt to the acceleration curve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Left Arrow | Steer left |
| A | Steer left |
| Right Arrow | Steer right |
| D | Steer right |

## Tips & Tricks

- **Speed increases over time** — don't get comfortable. What feels manageable at the start becomes a reflex test within 30 seconds. Stay mentally engaged from the very first second.
- **Hug the center when obstacles cluster near the edges.** Most obstacles are placed to push you toward one side. The middle of the slope is usually the safest corridor.
- **React early, not late.** By the time an obstacle looks threatening, it's almost too late to dodge it. Train yourself to read the slope two or three beats ahead.
- **Short sessions help you learn patterns.** Each run lasts under a minute at first. Use that to your advantage — rapid repetition builds muscle memory faster than long, frustrated sessions.
- **Don't overthink it.** Slope rewards flow state over calculated analysis. Once you start second-guessing every turn, you tense up and your reaction time drops. Trust your instincts and let the run carry you.

<Note>
  The game loads as a Unity WebGL bundle — give it 15–30 seconds to finish loading before the ball appears. If the screen stays black after that, try refreshing the page.
</Note>
