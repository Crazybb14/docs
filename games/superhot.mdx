SuperHot is unlike any other shooter you've played. Time moves only when you move — the moment you stop, the world freezes around you. Bullets hang in mid-air. Enemies pause mid-swing. That single mechanic transforms every level into a tactical puzzle: you can study the battlefield, plan your next three moves, then execute them in a blur of motion. It's part FPS, part puzzle game, and completely unlike anything else available in the browser.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Game">
    Open the game in a new browser tab from the homepage and wait for the game to load. Once the main menu appears, start a new game to enter the first level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Understand the Core Mechanic">
    Time in SuperHot advances only when you move your character or your mouse. The moment you stop all input, everything around you freezes — enemies stop, bullets halt in place, and you have all the time in the world to think.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assess the Level Before Moving">
    When you spawn into a level, take a moment to look around without moving. Identify how many red crystal enemies there are, where they're positioned, and whether any bullets are already in flight toward you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move Deliberately to Dodge and Eliminate">
    Move in short, controlled bursts. Step sideways to let a bullet pass harmlessly by. Pick up a weapon by walking over it, then aim and fire. You can also throw weapons — a mid-air object distracts and staggers enemies, creating an opening.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear All Enemies to Complete the Level">
    Eliminate every red enemy in the level to advance. If you take a hit, the level resets. Use the pause-plan-execute loop: stop, assess, move.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| W / A / S / D | Move (also advances time) |
| Mouse | Aim and look around |
| Left Click | Shoot or punch |
| R | Reload |
| F | Pick up a weapon from the ground |
| Q | Throw the weapon you're holding |
| E | Interact |

## Tips & Tricks

- **Slow down to plan each move before you make it.** SuperHot rewards patience over speed. Standing still is free — use those frozen moments to map out exactly where you'll move, who you'll hit first, and how you'll avoid the bullets in the air.
- **Throw weapons when you run out of ammo.** A thrown gun or object staggers enemies it hits, giving you a brief opening to close in and punch or grab their weapon. Mid-air objects are some of the most powerful tools in the game.
- **Dodge bullets by moving sideways, not backward.** Bullets travel in a straight line. Stepping left or right past a bullet's path is far more effective than trying to outrun it by backing away.
- **The pause-plan-execute loop is the core skill.** Stop moving → plan your next sequence of moves → execute quickly → stop again. Mastering this rhythm turns seemingly impossible scenarios into clean, satisfying solutions.
- **Don't be afraid to take it one enemy at a time.** It's tempting to try to clear a whole room in one sweeping motion. Most failed runs happen because of overcommitting. Handle the nearest threat first, then re-assess with a full stop.

<Tip>
  SuperHot's core mechanic means you can always pause time by standing completely still — use it whenever you feel overwhelmed, even mid-dodge. The game will wait for you.
</Tip>
