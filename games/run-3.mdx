Run 3 takes the endless runner into deep space. You sprint through a crumbling tunnel suspended in the void, leaping over gaps and rotating the entire tunnel beneath your feet by running on the walls. Fall through a gap and you drift off into space — game over. With multiple playable characters, each with unique abilities, and an Explore mode featuring 50+ hand-crafted levels alongside an Infinite mode for endless play, Run 3 has more depth than almost any other browser runner out there.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Game and Choose a Mode">
    Open the game in a new browser tab from the homepage and select your mode from the main menu. **Explore Mode** takes you through a branching tunnel map with individual levels and a story. **Infinite Mode** generates a random, never-ending tunnel for a pure high-score challenge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Character">
    In Explore mode, you start as the default Runner. As you complete levels, you unlock new characters — each with different jump heights, speed, and special abilities. The Skater moves fast; the Lizard can run on walls more easily; the Student has multiple jumps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run and Jump Through the Tunnel">
    Use the **Left and Right Arrow keys** to move across the tunnel floor. Press **Up Arrow or Space** to jump over gaps. The goal is simple: don't fall into space.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rotate the Tunnel by Running on the Walls">
    This is Run 3's signature mechanic: if you run onto a wall, the entire tunnel rotates so that wall becomes the new floor. Use this to navigate around gaps that would otherwise be impossible to jump — run up the side and let the level rotate to solid ground.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Progress Through Explore Mode or Chase a High Score">
    In Explore mode, complete each level to unlock the next one on the tunnel map. In Infinite mode, your score is determined by how far you travel — push yourself to beat your best distance each run.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Left Arrow | Move left across the tunnel |
| Right Arrow | Move right across the tunnel |
| Up Arrow | Jump |
| Space | Jump (alternative) |
| Running on a wall | Rotates the tunnel — that wall becomes the new floor |

## Tips & Tricks

- **Use the tunnel rotation to your advantage, not as a last resort.** Don't wait until you're about to fall to run onto a wall. Proactively rotate the tunnel so gaps are already behind you before they become a threat.
- **Each character plays differently — experiment.** The Runner is a solid all-around starting pick, but the Student's multi-jump ability makes many difficult levels significantly easier. If you're stuck on a level, try switching characters.
- **Commit to a direction.** One of the most common mistakes is hesitating mid-rotation. Once you start running toward a wall to rotate the tunnel, commit fully — second-guessing mid-step sends you into a gap.
- **In Infinite mode, stay near the center of the tunnel.** Gaps can appear on either side without warning. Keeping your position central gives you more reaction time to jump or rotate before falling.
- **Explore mode tracks your progress across sessions.** Your completed levels are saved, so you can return to Explore mode at any point and pick up exactly where you left off without replaying finished levels.

<Info>
  Run 3 has two distinct modes: **Explore** (a level-based story mode with a branching tunnel map and 50+ levels) and **Infinite** (an endless randomly generated tunnel for high-score runs). Your Explore mode progress is saved automatically between sessions.
</Info>
