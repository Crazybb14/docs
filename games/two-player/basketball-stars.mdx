Basketball Stars puts two players head-to-head in a slick 1v1 basketball match, all on the same screen and the same keyboard. You dribble past your opponent, line up your shot, unleash a super move, and do everything you can to stop them from scoring on your end. The controls are simple enough to pick up in seconds, but mastering positioning and timing takes real practice.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your mode">
    Select **2 Player** from the main menu so both players share the same keyboard. Each player picks their character before tip-off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gain possession">
    Move toward the ball using your movement keys to pick it up. Whoever grabs the ball first goes on offense.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attack the basket">
    Drive toward your opponent's hoop using your movement keys. Use your dash to blow past a defender and get an open lane to the basket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shoot and score">
    Press your super shot key when you have a clear look at the basket. Well-timed shots from good positions have a higher chance of going in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play defense">
    When your opponent has the ball, position yourself between them and your basket. Block their shot attempts by jumping into their path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Win the match">
    The player with the most points when the timer runs out wins the match. First to the winning score in a timed period takes the game.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controls

| Action | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD | Arrow Keys |
| Super shot / action | G | L |
| Dash | H | K |

## Tips & Strategy

- **Stay between your opponent and the basket.** Good defensive positioning forces your opponent into tough shots rather than easy layups.
- **Save your super shot for open looks.** The super shot is most effective when you have space — using it while a defender is right in front of you wastes the move.
- **Don't hold the ball too long.** Standing still with the ball gives your opponent time to set their defense. Sprint past the defender as soon as you pick up the ball.
- **Use your dash offensively and defensively.** Dashing isn't just for attacking — you can also use it to close the gap on a ball carrier and force a turnover.
- **Watch the timer.** If you're ahead near the end of a match, playing keep-away by running in open space can force your opponent into a desperate gamble.

<Tip>
  Basketball Stars also has a 1-player mode against AI — use it to warm up before challenging a friend.
</Tip>
