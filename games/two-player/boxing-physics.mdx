Boxing Physics strips competitive gaming down to its most gloriously unhinged form: two ragdoll fighters, one button each, and a physics engine that decides who goes flying. Every punch sends your character flailing in an unpredictable direction, and the fighter who manages to stay upright long enough to knock their opponent down wins the round. There is no complicated moveset to memorize — just press W or Up Arrow and let the chaos unfold.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick your fighters">
    Choose your boxer from the character select screen. Each character has a different look but the core mechanic is the same for everyone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press your button to punch">
    Player 1 presses **W** and Player 2 presses **Up Arrow** to throw punches. Each press launches your fighter's arm in a wild swing controlled entirely by the physics engine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the physics decide">
    The ragdoll physics system takes over from the moment you press the button. Your punch might land clean, knock you off balance, or send both fighters stumbling — that unpredictability is the game.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Knock your opponent down">
    Land enough hits to knock your opponent off their feet. A fighter who falls and can't recover loses the round.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Win the match">
    The game is played over several rounds. The player who wins the most rounds takes the match. Expect rematches — no two rounds ever play out the same way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Player | Key |

## Tips & Strategy

- **Timing matters more than spamming.** Rapid mashing can work, but a well-timed single press when your opponent is off balance often lands a cleaner hit than a frantic flurry.
- **Watch your fighter's balance.** If your character is leaning or stumbling after a punch, pause briefly to let them recover their footing before swinging again. Punching while already off balance usually makes things worse.
- **Back off when you're falling.** If you find yourself going down, stop pressing the button for a moment. Continuing to punch while falling often accelerates the knockdown rather than helping you recover.
- **Different arenas, different floors.** Some stages have slippery or uneven surfaces that affect how your fighter's ragdoll behaves — what works on a solid floor may send you spinning on an icy one.
- **Use the ring edges.** Pressing your opponent toward the edge of the arena limits their room to recover and makes knockdowns easier to secure.
