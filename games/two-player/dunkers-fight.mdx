Dunkers Fight is a physics-based basketball game where you leap, stretch, and slam the ball into the hoop while simultaneously knocking your opponent out of the way. Your character moves like a ragdoll — press your key and watch your fighter lunge, flail, and somehow manage to dunk the ball in ways that look completely impossible. Each round lasts seconds, every point feels earned, and the rematch button will get a workout.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the match">
    Both players begin on opposite sides of the court. The ball spawns in the center — whoever gets to it first goes on offense.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press your key to jump and reach">
    Each player presses their single key to jump and stretch their character toward the ball. The ragdoll physics engine controls how your fighter moves once airborne.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grab the ball">
    Position your character so your jump carries you into the ball. Your fighter will grab and carry it toward the hoop if you time the leap correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Score the basket">
    Guide the ball into your opponent's hoop by jumping toward it. A successful dunk or toss earns you a point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play defense">
    When your opponent has the ball, jump between them and your hoop to block or intercept their attempt before it reaches the basket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach the winning score">
    The first player to reach the set score limit wins the match. Rounds are fast — expect to play several matches in a row.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips & Strategy

- **Jump when the ball is near your side.** Timing your press to coincide with the ball being at your reach level gives your ragdoll fighter the best chance of making contact.
- **Use the walls to bounce.** The ball ricochets off the side walls, and an experienced player can use wall bounces to send the ball toward the hoop from unexpected angles.
- **Rapid taps for frantic movement.** Hammering your key quickly produces short, rapid movements that can be more controllable than single long holds when the ball is right next to you.
- **Block by leaping between your opponent and the hoop.** If your opponent has the ball and is heading for your basket, jump early to put your fighter between them and the scoring zone — even a ragdoll block can deflect the ball.
- **Don't overcommit.** Jumping too aggressively when the ball is far away leaves your side of the court open for a quick score by your opponent.

<Tip>
  Dunkers Fight rounds are short and frantic — best played as a series of quick matches.
</Tip>
