Bloons TD is the tower defense game that turned balloon-popping into a full-blown strategy genre. Waves of bloons — balloons of varying colors, sizes, and toughness — travel along a fixed path toward the exit, and your job is to place monkey towers along the route to pop them before they escape. Earn money for every bloon you pop, spend it on new towers and upgrades, and survive as many rounds as you can. It is deceptively deep, endlessly replayable, and as satisfying now as it was the first time you played it.

Unblockedgames hosts three versions of the game: **Bloons TD 1**, **Bloons TD 2**, and **Bloons TD 4**. All three are Flash titles and run through the Ruffle emulator with no plugin required.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Survey the map">
    Each level shows a fixed path that bloons follow from the entrance to the exit. Study the path layout before placing your first tower — curves and chokepoints are prime real estate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place your first towers">
    Click a tower type from the panel, then click a valid spot on the map (not on the path) to place it. Towers automatically attack bloons within their range once a round begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the round">
    Click the Start Round button to send the first wave of bloons down the path. Watch how your towers perform and identify gaps in your coverage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn money and reinvest">
    Every bloon you pop earns cash. Between rounds, use that money to place new towers in weak spots or upgrade existing towers to improve their range, attack speed, or damage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle special bloon types">
    As rounds progress, tougher bloon types appear — lead bloons that can only be popped by explosives, camo bloons invisible to most towers, and ceramic bloons that absorb many hits. Make sure your tower lineup can handle each type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protect your lives">
    Every bloon that reaches the exit costs you a life. Lose all your lives and the game ends. Keep your defense tight at every point on the path, especially near the exit where escaping bloons do the most psychological damage.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tower Types (Bloons TD 4)

Bloons TD 4 has the largest tower roster of the three versions. Here is a quick overview of the core options:

| Tower | Description |
|---|---|
| **Dart Monkey** | Your basic all-purpose tower. Throws darts at nearby bloons. Cheap and effective in the early rounds. |
| **Tack Shooter** | Fires tacks in eight directions at once. Excellent at corners and curves where bloons bunch up. |
| **Bomb Tower** | Lobs explosive bombs that pop groups of bloons in a blast radius. Essential for lead bloons. |
| **Ice Tower** | Freezes all bloons in range briefly. Combos devastatingly well with bomb towers placed nearby. |
| **Monkey Buccaneer** | A boat tower for water tiles. Fires darts and can be upgraded to use cannonballs and additional crew. |
| **Super Monkey** | The most powerful tower in the game. Fires darts at blinding speed. Expensive, but worth saving up for on harder levels. |

## Tips & Strategy

- **Place tack shooters at tight curves.** A curve forces bloons to slow and bunch together — a tack shooter firing in all directions at that point pops far more bloons per shot than one placed on a straight stretch.
- **Upgrade a few towers deeply rather than buying many cheap ones.** A fully upgraded Dart Monkey covers more ground and deals more damage than three basic ones. Spreading your money thin leaves you with a weak overall defense.
- **Combo ice and bomb towers.** Frozen bloons take double damage from explosions. Place a bomb tower directly adjacent to an ice tower, and the combo will tear through even ceramic bloons with ease.
- **Save for a Super Monkey on hard levels.** The Super Monkey's attack speed is so high that a single well-placed one can hold an entire section of the path on its own. Prioritize it on rounds where large bloon counts overwhelm your other towers.
- **Bloons TD 4 is the most strategic version.** If you want the most tower variety, upgrade paths, and map complexity, start there. Bloons TD 1 and 2 are great for casual play and mastering the basics.
