Tetris is one of the most enduring puzzle games ever made, and it plays just as well in a browser tab as it did in any arcade cabinet or handheld console. Seven differently shaped tetrominoes — the I, O, T, S, Z, J, and L pieces — fall from the top of the screen one at a time, and your job is to rotate and position them to build complete horizontal lines. Clear lines to score points, survive as the pace increases, and see how long you can hold the stack at bay before it reaches the top.

Unblockedgames offers three variants: **Classic Tetris** (the standard single-player experience), **Twitch Tetris** (a fast HTML5/JavaScript implementation built for maximum input responsiveness), and **Tyrone's Tetris** (a Flash version of the game that runs via the Ruffle emulator).

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Watch the next piece">
    A preview window shows the upcoming tetromino. Use it to plan where the current piece should land before it arrives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move and rotate the falling piece">
    Use the Left and Right Arrow Keys to slide the piece horizontally and Up Arrow to rotate it. You have until the piece lands to find the right position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the piece">
    Lower the piece using the Down Arrow for a soft drop or Space for an instant hard drop. Hard drops lock the piece immediately — use them when you're confident in the placement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear complete lines">
    When every cell in a horizontal row is filled with blocks, that row is cleared and all pieces above it drop down. Clearing multiple lines at once scores significantly more points.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Survive the speed increase">
    The game increases the drop speed every time you advance a level. Each new level demands faster decisions and tighter placements. The game ends when the stack reaches the top of the board.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Left / Right Arrow | Move piece left or right |
| Up Arrow | Rotate piece |
| Down Arrow | Soft drop (speed up fall) |
| Space | Hard drop (instantly place piece) |

## Tips & Strategy

- **Keep the stack as flat as possible.** A flat, even surface gives you the most options for placing the next piece. A jagged stack full of peaks and valleys will bury you in awkward situations.
- **Never leave covered gaps.** A hole with a block on top is extremely difficult to clear because every piece above that gap must be removed before the hole can be filled — and removing them just adds to the mess.
- **Reserve a column for the I-piece.** The most efficient strategy in Tetris is to build a tall, flat stack with one column kept empty on either side. When the long I-piece arrives, drop it into that column to clear four lines at once — a move called a Tetris.
- **Plan two or three moves ahead.** Once you're comfortable with basic placement, start positioning the current piece with the next piece in mind. The preview window is your most important tool.
- **Speed up intentionally on clear sections.** Using hard drops on easy placements saves time and gives you more thinking time when a tricky piece arrives.

<Tip>
  A "Tetris" is clearing 4 lines at once with an I-piece — it's worth the most points of any single move. Keep one column free along the edge of your stack to set it up.
</Tip>
