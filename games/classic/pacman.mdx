Pacman is the arcade maze game that needs no introduction. You guide a yellow, dot-chomping character through a winding maze while four colored ghosts hunt you down. Eat every dot in the maze to advance to the next level, pick up power pellets to briefly flip the dynamic and eat the ghosts yourself, and rack up the highest score you can before your three lives run out. Simple to learn, genuinely difficult to master — and completely free to play in your browser right now.

## How to Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate the maze">
    Use the Arrow Keys to steer Pacman through the maze. Pacman moves continuously in the last direction you pressed — tap a new direction to turn at the next available corridor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Eat all the dots">
    Every small white dot you eat scores points and counts toward clearing the level. You must eat every single dot in the maze to complete the level and advance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Avoid the four ghosts">
    Blinky (red), Pinky (pink), Inky (cyan), and Clyde (orange) roam the maze and will kill you on contact. Each ghost has a unique movement pattern — learning those patterns is the key to surviving longer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use power pellets to fight back">
    The four large flashing dots in the corners of the maze are power pellets. Eating one temporarily turns all four ghosts blue and vulnerable — chase them down and eat them for bonus points before the effect wears off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the maze and advance">
    Eat every dot and power pellet to complete the level. The maze resets with the same layout but the ghosts move faster, making each subsequent level more challenging.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips & Strategy

- **Learn the ghost AI patterns.** Blinky directly chases Pacman's current position. Pinky targets four tiles ahead of Pacman's direction. Inky's path is unpredictable and depends on both Pacman and Blinky's positions. Clyde chases Pacman when far away but retreats to his corner when he gets too close — use this to your advantage.
- **Use the corner tunnels.** The tunnels on the left and right sides of the maze teleport you to the opposite side. Ghosts slow down when entering the tunnels, so using them when a ghost is chasing you buys you valuable time.
- **Save power pellets for groups.** Eating a power pellet when only one ghost is nearby wastes most of its potential. Wait until two or more ghosts are clustered near a pellet, then eat it and collect multiple ghosts for exponentially higher points.
- **Patrol the edges first.** Clearing the outer corridors early gives you more open space to maneuver through the middle of the maze when the ghosts close in.
- **Plan your escape routes.** Before committing to a corridor, glance at where each ghost is heading — if an exit is already blocked, a corner can become a dead end fast.

<Tip>
  Eating ghosts during a power pellet gives increasing points: 200, 400, 800, and 1600 for each consecutive ghost you eat without letting the effect expire.
</Tip>
