The Unblockedgames homepage gives you two fast ways to find exactly what you want to play: you can search for a game by name, or filter the entire library by category. Both methods update the game grid instantly, so you never have to dig through pages or wait for results to load.

## Search Bar

At the top of the homepage, you'll find a search bar. As you type, the game grid filters in real-time to show only titles that match what you've entered — no need to press Enter or click a search button. The results update character by character, so you can often find your game after just a few keystrokes.

For example, type **"slope"** to jump straight to Slope, or type **"mario"** to surface any Mario titles in the library. The more specific you are, the narrower the results — but a single keyword is usually all you need.

<Tip>
  Search is case-insensitive, so it doesn't matter how you capitalize your query. "SLOPE", "slope", and "Slope" all return the same results.
</Tip>

## Category Filters

If you'd rather browse than search, the category dropdown lets you filter the entire game library by type. Select a category and the grid immediately refreshes to show only matching games.

| Category | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every game available on the platform — the full unfiltered library. |
| **Popular** | Fan-favorite titles that see the most plays, including Slope, 1v1.lol, Minecraft, Retro Bowl, and SuperHot. |
| **Two Player** | Games that support local two-player mode, where two people share one keyboard on the same device. |
| **Streaming** | Links to movie and TV streaming sites for when you want to switch from gaming to watching. |

<Info>
  The **Two Player** filter surfaces games where two people can play simultaneously on a single device, splitting the keyboard between them. These are great for playing with a friend side by side.
</Info>

## Browsing the Grid

Whether you're searching or filtering by category, games are always displayed as a visual thumbnail grid. Each tile shows the game's icon and its name, making it easy to recognize titles at a glance. Scroll through the grid to explore what's available — you might discover a game you haven't tried before.

When you find something you want to play, click the tile. The game opens immediately in a new browser tab, ready to load.
